# Children vs Adults Survival Analysis

## Research Question
"Did children have a better survival rate than adults on the Titanic?"

## Methodology
- Defined children as passengers under 18 years old
- Used passengers with known ages only
- Compared survival rates between both two groups

## Findings
- Number of children: 139
- Number of adults: 575 
- Children survival rate: 0.5035971223021583 %
- Adults survival rate: 0.3826086956521739 %

## Conclusion
Children had significantly higher survival rates, confirming that evacuation protocols prioritized saving young lives.
